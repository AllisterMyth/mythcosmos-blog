# Marshall Mode Usb- C earphones Review

*   *   [Life](/categories/life)
    |
*   26 May, 2026 |
*   02 Mins read

I am mainly writing this as I could not find any good reviews for this product when I was purchasing it. I will try to cover everything (looks, build, price, sound quality) as much as possible and provide a comparison with other IEMs.

## LOOKS

It’s fairly good looking, not as artistic as Tangzu Waner but still very sleek with gold and black colour. It’s almost identical to the OG Marshall Mode EQ except for the usb-C. I like it but then again looks never bother me much.

## BUILD

The build quality is really good for the earbuds compared to other IEMs in a similar bracket. Yet the wire feels a little thin. The usb-C port is connected nicely so chances of wire breaking at joint are low. At some point, I was really worried about the longevity of my IEMs but after a lot of research, I came to the conclusion that the more you use them the faster they die and generally last 1-3 years for heavy users like me. Chifi IEMs often last shorter. I now consider buying them more of a service than a product with the warranty lasting nearly its lifespan.

## PRICE

I got these for 4500 INR for a year long warranty from Amazon but it’s better to get from the official site at nearly the same price for a 2 year warranty. They are at the edge of this bracket of IEMs.

## SOUND

Finally the main factor. In short they sound great. They have a very V-shaped curve (high bass, high treble). The bass is tight and sound is clear. I will link the sound curve at the bottom for technical people. The equaliser works for at least a range of 5db at all bands without any noticeable drop in audio quality. Since the wire is not detachable, that decreases noise a lot. The impedance is low so it actually feels like a speaker is going off in your head. I personally dislike chasing sound signatures of IEMs rather than the music itself so it’s great for me. In general, I would consider this the best IEM to get into music. Click [https://squig.link/lab/hana/?share=Mode\_EQ,5128\_DF\_Target](https://squig.link/lab/hana/?share=Mode_EQ,5128_DF_Target) for the sound curve.
